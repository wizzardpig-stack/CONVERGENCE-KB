# CONVERGENCE Domain & Concept Taxonomy

## Primary user-facing domains
- SELF — identity, temperament, emotional/cognitive style, presentation
- MONEY — earning, resources, risk, security, acquisition, financial blind spots
- LOVE — attraction, bonding, partnership, conflict, relationship needs
- PURPOSE — vocation, direction, meaning, contribution, developmental themes
- SHADOW — self-sabotage, fear, defensive patterns, stress expression, contradictions
- TIME — cycles, periods, returns, dashas, profections, timing windows

## Secondary domains
CAREER, FAMILY, CREATIVITY, SPIRITUALITY, SEX_INTIMACY, SOCIAL, COMPATIBILITY, LOCATION, HEALTH_SYMBOLISM.

## Concept rules
Concepts are normalized lower_snake_case labels used to connect unlike systems without pretending their doctrines are identical.

Examples:
- autonomy
- security
- novelty_seeking
- structure
- intimacy
- emotional_intensity
- analytical_focus
- leadership
- visibility
- status_motivation
- service_orientation
- risk_tolerance
- conflict_style
- attachment_need
- discipline
- adaptability
- communication
- creativity
- material_focus
- spiritual_orientation

## Synthesis guardrail
A shared concept means two rules may speak to a similar practical theme. It does NOT mean the traditions have the same metaphysics, causal theory, or technical definition.

## Convergence
A synthesis may be called convergent only when:
1. at least two independently sourced rules map to the same normalized concept,
2. their triggering chart facts are independently present,
3. no unresolved chart-changing conflict invalidates either rule,
4. the prose states the degree of support without inventing scientific probability.

## Contradiction
Opposing concept polarities are surfaced rather than averaged away. Contradiction is a product feature, not an error.
